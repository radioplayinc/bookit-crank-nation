What does studio time cost at Crank Nation Studios?

Studio time is $60 per hour, with mixing and mastering included.

How much is mixing or mastering per song?

Mixing is $80 per song and mastering is $50 per song. Mix plus master is $130 per song.

What is an exclusive beat?

An exclusive beat is $200 per beat.
